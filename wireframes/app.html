Connection

Device connection method

Wi‑Fi (default)

Phone and PC on the same Wi‑Fi. Click Start extract, scan the QR (or open the URL), then pick files or folders on the phone. No USB cable. Move is not available — uploads are always copy into originals/. Allow inbound TCP on the PC firewall if prompted.

MTP

On the phone, choose File transfer / MTP (not charge-only). Windows and Linux usually need nothing else on the PC. macOS may need extra setup — follow any in-app hint if the device is not found.

ADB (cable)

Enable Developer options → USB debugging → accept the RSA prompt on the phone. SpaceMaker includes adb in the app — no separate Android SDK install.

About & Legal

SpaceMaker runs on your computer. Your media stays local unless you use the optional LAN gallery on your network.

Privacy

We do not operate a cloud that stores your photos or videos. Device access and conversion logs stay on your machine. v1: no analytics telemetry.

Contact: contact@daniel-ir.eu

Third-party tools

On first launch, SpaceMaker downloads pinned builds of these programs into a folder on your computer (not inside the app). If a download fails, install the tool with your package manager; SpaceMaker uses that copy when it is on your PATH.

  • FFmpeg / ffprobe — video
  • ImageMagick — AVIF
  • ExifTool — metadata
  • libmtp — MTP
  • Android platform-tools (adb)
